Project 01

Client 1 — Sales Optimisation

Retail & Consumer Goods · MS Excel
⚠ Problem
Leadership had no visibility into which regions drove revenue, which age groups were most valuable, which products performed best, or when demand peaked — resulting in misallocated budgets, inventory stockouts, and missed promotional opportunities.
✓ Solution
Analysed 113,058 transactions across 6 countries using Excel pivot tables and built a fully interactive dashboard with KPI cards, dynamic charts, and slicers — enabling leadership to filter by region, demographic, and season in one view.
$95M Total Revenue $42M P&L 113K Transactions 1.35M Units Sold
  • United States identified as highest-revenue market; France & Germany flagged as growth opportunities
  • Adults (35+) drive 40% of total revenue — confirmed as primary target demographic
  • Road Bikes generate highest margin; spring/summer seasonality mapped for demand forecasting
Excel Pivot Tables Dashboard Design Customer Segmentation Demand Forecasting
Project 02

Client 2 — Customer & Product Insights

Digital Retail · SQL + Power BI
⚠ Problem
The client collected rich transactional data across 5 tables but had no analytical capability to use it. Leadership couldn't identify profitable bundles, understand loyalty behaviour, track payment failures, or connect support ratings to service problems.
✓ Solution
Wrote 5 targeted SQL queries in PostgreSQL to answer critical business questions, then built an interactive Power BI dashboard connected live to the database — giving NovaMart its first data strategy blueprint.
$190M Revenue $158K Avg Spend 5 SQL Queries
  • Bundle J led subscriptions (2,232); Wellness drove $75.3M — top revenue category
  • Card payments had highest failure rate (345) — Wallet promotion recommended
  • Loyalty tiers show near-identical spend — restructured reward system recommended
PostgreSQL SQL Power BI KPI Design
Project 03

Client 3 — BACCM Framework

Private Healthcare · BACCM
⚠ Problem
A growing private healthcare facility relied entirely on phone calls for appointment booking — causing double-bookings, long patient wait times, overloaded reception staff, and a hard ceiling on the centre's capacity to scale.
✓ Solution
Applied the BACCM framework to map all 6 stakeholder groups and proposed a digital self-service booking platform with automated reminders and staff reallocation — transitioning the centre from reactive to proactive appointment management.
6 Stakeholder Groups 4 Proposed Solutions
  • Reframed the problem beyond a tech upgrade — identified systemic bottleneck eroding growth
  • Web & mobile self-service booking proposed with real-time doctor availability
  • Automated SMS/email reminders designed to reduce no-shows and free reception capacity
BACCM Stakeholder Analysis Requirements Definition Solution Design
Project 04

Client 4 — HR Transformation

FMCG / Manufacturing · Business Case
⚠ Problem
75,000+ employees across 5 continents operated on fragmented, independently-built HR systems — making global workforce reporting impossible, creating compliance risk across multiple regulatory environments, and driving disproportionate IT costs.
✓ Solution
Evaluated 3 enterprise HR platforms, recommended SAP SuccessFactors, and produced a formal business case for the Global Board — covering financial analysis, ROI, a 7-risk assessment, and SAFe Agile justification for a phased 5-continent rollout.
$8.97M Investment +$4.5M Net Return 24–30 Mo Payback
  • $4.5M annual benefit from IT consolidation, HR automation & compliance savings identified
  • SAP SuccessFactors selected for 100+ country compliance and proven FMCG track record
  • 7 risks assessed with phased migration, sandbox testing and SAFe governance as mitigations
Business Case ROI Analysis Risk Assessment SAFe / Agile SDLC
Project 05 · Capstone

Client 5 — Canteen Ordering System

FMCG / Internal Operations · Full BA Delivery
⚠ Problem
The company has 1,500 employees were spending 30–35 minutes queuing for lunch daily, leaving only 10–15 minutes to actually eat. The canteen simultaneously overproduced some items while running out of others — costing the business £4,062,500 in annual lost productivity.
✓ Solution
Led full BA delivery of an Online Canteen Ordering System — the only option that recovers the entire waiting window without employees leaving their desks. Delivered all 8 BA workstreams: stakeholder matrix, business case, RACI, 10 system features, swimlane process map, 10 user stories with acceptance criteria, and Jira sprint board.
£16,250 Daily Loss Recovered 752% ROI £500K Project Cost 8 BA Deliverables
  • Rejected third-party apps and canteen expansion through structured financial & operational analysis
  • Defined 10 system features across ordering, inventory, delivery, payments and payroll integration
  • Organised 10 user stories into 3 Jira sprints covering 9 stakeholder groups
Stakeholder Analysis Business Case RACI Matrix Process Mapping User Stories Jira Draw.io
